# Separate energized load from contractual capacity

Method update: October 18, 2026. Proposed experiments below are not reported as executed results.

The current ERCOT fixture separates announced and observed energized MW. A richer refresh should keep requested MW, approved interconnection MW, contracted supply, energized connection and observed load as distinct states. These quantities describe different parts of delivery and cannot substitute for one another in the same score.

A synthetic campus can hold a 100 MW power contract while only 20 MW of its connection is energized. The contract does not raise observed energized MW to 100. A further 10 MW curtailment pledge also does not establish 10 MW of dispatchable relief until activation rules, baseline, duration and metered response are known.

The next proposed fixture should move one project through these states, with an evidence date and source per transition. Its comparison report should identify an observed state change separately from a scoring-rule change. Generator-queue rules must remain scoped to generators; FERC's Order 2023 explainer does not itself settle a large-load project's eligibility. Leave restricted portal acceptance and registration to the existing human access path.
